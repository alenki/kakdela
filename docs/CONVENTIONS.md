# «Как дела?»: frontend conventions

The site reads like a well-set printed café menu: warm paper, black ink, one red. The character comes from four things only: the real logo with its red clover, heavy condensed display type, real food photography, and one interaction (the hero's tear-off notice). Everything else stays quiet, aligned and the same from section to section. A section has at most one signature moment. When in doubt, remove it. Four are declared exceptions, each on the terms of its own section below: the coffee poster, the weekly puzzle, the runner and the cafés plan (`/locations`); so are the feedback page's note (see «Обратная связь»), the about page's hanging sign (see «О нас») and the menu's notice turned over and its table (see «Что будете?»).

## Stack
- Next.js 16 App Router, React 19.3, TypeScript, Tailwind v4 (CSS-first), GSAP 3.15 with `@gsap/react`, Motion 13, Lenis.
- Read `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` before using a Next API. In Next 16:
  - `next/image` uses `preload`, not `priority`;
  - `images.qualities` is `[75, 85]`.
- Browsers: `package.json`'s `browserslist` is Next's default with Safari lowered from 16.4 to 16.0, so the bundle also parses on iPhones on iOS 16.0 to 16.3 (the build compiles class `static {}` blocks away). Nothing that ships to the browser may use a regex lookbehind: Safari before 16.4 can't parse one, and a single regex literal with it stops the whole bundle (`typograf.ts` has none, and a test keeps it that way).

- Each section:
  - is `src/components/sections/<Name>.tsx`, a **server component** that renders all copy;

The English site is the same code built with `NEXT_PUBLIC_LOCALE=en` (docs/DEPLOY.md): a string added to `ru.ts` needs its twin in `src/i18n/en.ts`, and new content words their English in `src/content/en`; `src/i18n/en.test.ts` fails until they are there.
- Brand components live in `src/components/brand/*`: `Wordmark` (full | compact | badge; the only place the octagon and the clover appear, outside the coffee poster), `Photo` and `Slip` (the paper slip, below). - `DialogueHeading` is in `src/components/type/DialogueHeading.tsx`. Every section heading uses it.

## Design tokens (globals.css)
- **Colours:** `cream` (page), `milk` (the rare raised surface: the hero notice, the torn tab that lands in its answer, the team's slips, the reviews' 5,0 slip and notes, the puzzle's grade, and the cafés plan's sheet), `ink` (text, and the dark sections' background), `graphite` (secondary text on cream), `tomato` / `tomato-ink` (the one accent). Tomato is for the logo clover, focus and at most one small highlight per viewport. It is never a section background (except the coffee poster) and never body text. No other colours (the coffee poster adds its cup's cobalt, scoped to `.coffee`).
- **Themes:** `data-theme="cream"` or `data-theme="ink"` on `section.field` (and `"tomato"` for the coffee poster alone). Hero through reviews are cream, except the coffee poster; evening, chess, game and footer are ink (the page is one café day and ends at night). Never put `data-theme` on anything inside a section: the header and the action bar take the last themed element under their line. Each theme sets `--bg`, `--fg`, `--fg-soft`, `--accent`, `--accent-ink` (red for small text, lifted on ink), `--focus` and `--grain`. `.field` paints them; `.field::before` is the grain, `::after` is free. Two cream sections in a row get a gutter-to-gutter hairline automatically, so don't add a top border. The header, the action bar and the menu dialog take the theme of the section beneath them.
- **Type sizes:** `--fs-body`, `--fs-small` (.875rem), `--fs-label` (.8125rem), `--lead`, `--h2` (the one section-title size), `--h3` (item titles). No per-section poster sizes, except the coffee answer.
- **Spacing:** `.section-pad` (`--section-pad`, bottom 1.15× the top), `--head-gap` (heading block → content), `--foot-gap` (a list or grid → the closing line under it: links, a note, the pie; the same step in every section), `--gutter`, `--grid-gap`, `--header-h`.
- **Lines:** `--line` (16% of the text colour), `--line-strong` (28%), `--rule` (1px). Use `.rule-t` / `.rule-b`. No heavier rules (the coffee card's 1.5px ink border is the exception).
- **Viewport heights:** size with `var(--vh)` and `var(--svh)` (1% of 100vh and of 100svh, frozen per width by the head script: `11 * var(--vh)` inside `clamp()`/`min()`/`calc()`, `calc(3 * var(--vh))` on its own), never raw `vh`/`svh`. Telegram's in-app browser resizes the page as its bars fold, so raw units reflow the whole page mid-scroll, and the height change would make the page refresh ScrollTrigger, whose scroll restore stops iOS's momentum dead. `dvh` only for a fixed overlay that must follow the live screen (the «Разделы» dialog).
- **Radius:** 2px for photos and panels, 999px for buttons and toggles only.
- **Shadows:** none, except the paper shadow (`--paper-shadow`) under the hero notice, its landed tab, the team's slips, the reviews' 5,0 slip and notes, the puzzle's grade, the cafés plan's sheet, the about page's sign, and the coffee poster's flat offset shadows (the cup, the card).
- **Paper:** `--paper`, `--paper-tex`, `--paper-shadow` and `--edge` are global tokens: the hero notice, its torn strips, the team's slips, the reviews' slip and notes, the cafés plan's sheet and the about page's sign are one paper.
- **Layout:** every section is full viewport width. `.wrap` has no max width; it only adds `--gutter` (16px on phones, `clamp(24px, 3.4vw, 104px)` from 640px). Desktop is a 12-column grid from 1024px, gutter to gutter, with `--grid-gap` column gaps. Compose for 390, 1440, 1920 and 2560 alike: no centred column, no dead halves, never horizontal scroll. The one centred composition is «Отзывы»: its pile of notes on columns 3 to 10, the arrows centred under it. Running text keeps a measure (≤ 60ch; `.lead` 44ch). `.bleed`, `.bleed-l` and `.bleed-r` break imagery out to the screen edge; text always keeps the gutter. Past 1600px the root font size grows (16px → 20px at 2560px), so anything in rem scales with the canvas.

## Type
Two families, plus the barista's hand in one place:
- **Display** (Sofia Sans Extra Condensed): `.display`, heavy (860) uppercase, line-height .9. Section titles, dish names, big numbers. Pair `.item-title` (`--h3`) with it for dish and item names.
- **Sans** (Montserrat, the café's own face): body, UI, buttons, labels, prices, hours, phones.
  - `.mono` is Montserrat with tabular figures, for prices, hours and phones. **₽ is never set in the display face.**
  - `.label` is small Montserrat 500 in `--fg-soft`, sentence case, no uppercase, no tracking.
  - `.lead` is the section lead: sans at `--lead`, `--fg-soft`, 44ch, `text-wrap: balance` (two to four even lines, usually broken at the dash).
  - `.reply` is the café's voice: Montserrat italic (400; running text is 450, `--fw-text`), only for the team statement, the review quotes, the hero's reply line, the coffee poster's reply and the reply under the puzzle's grade. At most one italic moment per section; never for captions, labels, buttons or notes. No serif anywhere.
- **Hand** (Neucha, `--font-hand`, not preloaded): only the barista's notes in the coffee poster. Nowhere else.
- **The logo's lettering** (`src/lib/lettering.ts`) is not a font: the wordmark's К, А, Д, Е and Л redrawn from the trace, and the letters it lacks drawn from the same parts (hairline uprights, heavy bands and «/» strokes, hairline «\», legs that curl into a ball). Only the about page's question is set in it, as SVG on the server with the words beside it for screen readers. A question with a letter it doesn't have fails the build: draw the letter there first.

Headings get `text-wrap: balance`, paragraphs `pretty`. Keep «ёлочки».

## Section heading
```tsx
<header className="section-head">
  <DialogueHeading id="zavtraki-title" q="Завтраки" a="Что на завтрак?" />
  <p className="lead">…</p>
</header>
```
- `q` is the section's plain name (not shown; it is the title when there is no `a` and no children); `a` is the title in display type at `--h2`. Other props: `as` (`h2` | `h3`), `id`, `className`, `answerClassName` (on the title), `children` (after the title, inside the heading), and `ask`: a question line over the title in the thin display voice (`.dlg-ask`), used only by the coffee poster («А кофе?»), so its heading reads «А кофе? А с кофе мы не шутим.».
- It renders `h2.dlg > [span.dlg-ask] + span.dlg-title.display`. `data-dlg-q` (the `ask` line) and `data-dlg-a` (the title) are motion hooks.
- The label is `--fs-small` up to a laptop and then grows with the title (about 1:6.6 from 1440 to 2560), so it doesn't get lost under a 150px title.
- From 1024px the title spans columns 1–8 and the lead sits in 9–12 on the title's last baseline; phones stack. `--head-gap` below is included. The one exception is «Уже спрашивали.» (`/feedback`): from 1440 its title takes 1–6 and its lead 7–12, over the list's right column (see «Обратная связь»).
- One section passes children instead of a title: Team (its italic statement). «Отзывы» asks in its title («Как вам у нас?») and the flagship's 5,0 answers on a slip beside it, on the title's line (`.rv-head`; the slip goes under the title where the line is too short). The slip is only paper to a screen reader, so an `.sr-only` line after the heading says whose score it is.
- **No em dashes, anywhere**: not in copy, alt text, titles, screen reader text, comments or docs, and not as dialogue marks (the hero's exchange is «Как дела?» / «Сонно.» with no dash). Use a comma, a colon, a full stop or «·» instead, or rephrase.

## Components
- **Photo:** `<Photo photo={p} sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 33vw, 100vw" aspect="4/5" />`, also `className`, `preload` (the one LCP image only) and `loading`.
  - Rectangular, 2px radius, `object-fit: cover`, blur placeholder. No frames, no octagons (the coffee card's `OctPhoto` is the one exception).
  - `aspect` is `"w/h"` (default `"4/5"`) and sets `--photo-aspect`. Keep it true to the rendered shape; a section may override `aspect-ratio` in CSS or set it to `auto` with a height.
  - `sizes` describes the frame's width; `coverSizes` scales it when the source is wider than the frame.
  - Where a point of the photo must land in a set place (a café's sign), `FramedPhoto` (`src/components/about`) lays it by a crop from `src/lib/crop.ts` instead of `object-fit`. A café photo's `sign` in `photos.ts` is where its «Как дела?» sign is (the middle of the line, at the clover, and its width, as fractions of the photo): re-measure it when the photo changes.
  - Stock stand-ins (`placeholder: true` in `photos.ts`) get a quiet «Фото временное» badge, hidden from screen readers (the alt text already says so). Add `photo--sm` on thumbnails about 96–128px wide for a smaller badge.
- **Buttons:** `<a className="btn btn-primary"><BtnLabel>Смотреть меню</BtnLabel></a>` and `<a className="btn"><BtnLabel>…</BtnLabel></a>`. Pill, 48px tall, Montserrat 500. The primary is filled with the text colour and inverts on ink; the secondary has a `--line-strong` border. At most two in a group; prefer one button plus text links.
  - **The pour:** hover or a press (a mouse, or a pen; never touch) or keyboard focus fills the pill from the bottom with one flat liquid: the text colour into the secondary (ink on cream, milk on ink), tomato ink into the primary (milk words). No second tone, wash, highlight or gradient: the realism is all in the surface's shape and motion. The liquid is simulated (`src/lib/pour.ts`, tested in `pour.test.ts`): the pill is a 4 cm tank, poured into near the end wall on the pointer's side (the further in the pointer came, the further in it lands; keyboard: the end the focus came from) with a first rush, then a gush that eases off toward the brim and comes in gulps, the level following its real cross-section. The stream hits the bare floor and shoots across it as a sheet, the wave it drives slams into the far wall and climbs it, then the pill tops up unevenly; where the stream lands, and at the sheet's head, the surface chops into short ripples that die as the pill fills. The surface is the tank's own sloshing modes at water's tempo for that depth; a face too steep for water spills. The drain runs out at the exit-side wall with a falling head and a lurch toward it (nothing climbs the wall it drains from). No two pours alike: every start or turn deals its own strength, landing, slant, run, gulps, chop and lurch from the pill's seed (`pour-controller.ts` gives each pill a random one; a seed replays exactly, so the tests pin theirs). About 0.5s in (under 0.6s on every pill, every seed; the label is covered in about 0.15s), about 0.43s out, reversible at any moment; the CSS fallback's flat level takes 560ms in and 460ms out. `BtnLabel`'s copy of the label sits inside `.btn__pour` and is clipped with it, so the words turn over exactly at the surface. A press sinks the pill to 0.965 and springs it back; it never drains it.
  - `pour-controller.ts` runs every pour: listeners delegated from `document`, one frame loop that runs only while a pour moves and stops the frame it is out of sight, and the only writer of `.btn__pour`'s inline `clip-path` (`none` brim-full, removed empty). Never give `.btn__pour` a React `style`, and never toggle a `.btn`'s classes from script (React owns them: the runner swaps `btn-primary`). Never promote `.btn__pour` to a layer of its own (`will-change`, a transform, opacity, a filter): its copy would raster apart from the real label and change weight at the surface. On hover the filled pill keeps its own edge (only the outline pill's border turns to the text colour).
  - Put everything visible inside `BtnLabel` (the chess CTA's «↗» too) and keep `.sr-only` text outside it: the copy is `aria-hidden`. Forced colours drop the pour. Whenever the simulation isn't running (no JS, before hydration, reduced motion) CSS fills the pill with a flat level instead (`html:not([data-pour])`), and reduced motion makes that an instant swap.
- **Text links:** `<a className="dlink">Все кофейни</a>`. Montserrat 500 at the surrounding size, a 1px underline at 35% that turns solid on hover (fine pointers) or press, and a 44px hit area on touch.
  - A group of links (a section's closing pair) goes in `<p className="dlinks">`: one line when they fit, otherwise stacked; on touch the rows are 44px apart, so the hit areas never overlap.
- **External links:**
  ```tsx
  <a className="dlink" href={…} target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">
    Telegram<span className="dlink__ext" aria-hidden="true">↗</span>
    <span className="sr-only"> {t.a11y.newTab}</span>
  </a>
  ```
- **Tags and features:** plain text joined by « · » in `.label`. No pill chips.
- **Menu rows:** name and price, no dotted leader (the coffee card is the exception: see «Coffee: the poster»).
  - Primary rows (breakfasts, desserts): the name in `.display.item-title`, the price in `.mono`, an optional description line below. Stacked lists (breakfasts) separate rows with 1px `--line` rules, make every row as tall as the tallest (`grid-auto-rows: 1fr` on the list) and set the price in a column of its own, centred vertically in the row beside the name and description.
  - Secondary rows in sans (the evening pie) use `.menu-row` from globals.css: the name may wrap and the price stands on its last line, at least 1.5rem from it (the hero's reply is the exception: its price stands centred beside the reply's lines):
    ```tsx
    <p className="menu-row">
      <span className="menu-row__name">{name}</span>
      <span className="menu-row__price mono">{rub(350)}</span>
    </p>
    ```
  - A city without a price shows `t.hero.askLater` («уточняйте в кофейне») instead.
- **Status:** `<CafeStatus cafe={c} />` renders `.status` as words only («открыто · до 23:00»): no dot, no green, no pulse.
- **City switch:** `<CitySwitch compact? labelledBy? />` is the segmented pill toggle. It is named «Город» unless `labelledBy` points at a visible label.
- **Clover:** only inside the logo (`Wordmark`). No clover bullets or decorations. The coffee poster is the exception: the cup's decal and the hatched clover behind it.
- **Paper slip:** a strip of the notice's paper with one word or number in display type, tilted (`--tilt`), with a torn end (`--tear`) and a lighter fringe along it (`--fibres`), all from `tornStrip(id)` in `src/lib/tear.ts`. Four exist, and all render through `<Slip seed label mood? className />` (`src/components/brand/Slip.tsx`, look in brand.css; the caller's class only places and sizes it): the tab that lands in the hero's answer, the team's answers (one on the foot of each portrait, each with its tab's tear), the reviews' 5,0 beside its title, and the puzzle's grade (the same answer's tab); the about page lays the «Лучше всех» slip across its photo and the eight answers at its end (`as="span"`, inside a radio's label). Its tilt lives in CSS `rotate`, never in a tweened `transform`: GSAP folds it into its transform during a landing and hands it back on `clearProps`.
- **Pile (reviews only):** the guests' words on notes of the notice's paper, torn off a pad along the top (`tornSheet(id)` in `src/lib/tear.ts`: the rip, its fringe and a small lean of the note's own), lying in a loose pile.
  - Every note is server-rendered and stacked in one grid cell, so the longest quote sets the pile's size. Where a note lies is its `data-pos` (0 on top) as CSS `translate` and `rotate` on the `li`: the server sets them in order and `ReviewsPile` moves them. Only the top note's words are shown, and read.
  - Next throws the top note aside and it slides back in under the pile while the others move up a place: the → pill, a click or a tap on the note, or a sideways drag let go past 90px or flicked (a mouse, a pen or a finger; up and down stays the page's scroll). While it is dragged, the note under it starts to come up. Back (the ← pill) pulls the bottom note out from under the pile and lays it on top. It loops, and it never moves on its own.
  - One motion per wrapper: the `li`'s place is CSS, the landing is `ReviewsMotion`'s on `.rv-note__land`, the throw and the drag are `ReviewsPile`'s on `.rv-note__fly`.
  - The pills are `.btn`s with `aria-controls`, and a polite live region reads «Отзыв 2 из 3: …» on each change. No dots: without JS the first note stays on top and the pills keep their place, unseen.

## Formatting and content
- Prices go through `rub()` from `src/lib/format.ts` (a no-break space and ₽). Counts go through `plural()` (via a `t.*` function in `ru.ts`), digit groups through `groupDigits()`.
- Keep copy plain and pass it through `typograf()` from `src/lib/typograf.ts` where it renders (no-break spaces after short words, before «·», in numbers and units, in addresses, and inside the brand's «как дела?»). `bindLastWord()` keeps a paragraph from ending on a lone word; `keepWhole()` keeps names whole (no break at a hyphen, none inside a short «…» name) for dish names, answers and quotes. `quote()` sets a guest's words in «…», with a name inside turned to „…“; it goes last: `quote(bindLastWord(keepWhole(typograf(text))))`. Take `NBSP` / `WJ` from there; don't write a local helper.
- Digit groups use a plain no-break space («2 709», «5 000 ₽»). Montserrat's narrow no-break space (U+202F) is too thin to see at label size, so don't use it.
- **City-specific content:** render **both cities on the server** and wrap each in `data-city-only="nalchik|vladikavkaz"`. CSS hides the inactive one (from `html[data-city]`, set before paint), so switching city never re-renders. `useKd((s) => s.city)` is only for things that must compute.
- Copy is plain, specific and warm, in the «вы» register. Keep the brand's dish names. No meta-instructions, asides, sign-offs or cute microcopy.

## Motion rules
- Client code imports GSAP from `@/lib/gsap`: `gsap`, `ScrollTrigger` and `useGSAP`, with the eases `kd.out`, `kd.inOut`, `kd.peel`, `kd.settle`, and `kd.unroll` and `kd.rollup` (the «Разделы» roll) registered. For the coffee poster it also registers `DrawSVGPlugin`.
- Allowed: one quiet entrance per block (opacity plus y 16–24px, `kd.out`, 0.6–0.8s, stagger ≤ 0.06, once), the hero's tear-off and its answer change, the breakfasts photo crossfade, the reviews' pile (the top note thrown aside, tilting as it goes, in 0.42s and back in under the pile in 0.6s, the others moving up a place in 0.56s, a dragged note following the pointer) and the one-time landing of its 5,0 slip and its notes (the hero's landing, the bottom note first), the team's slips landing on their portraits as each row rises (the hero's landing: y -26, scale 1.05, `kd.settle`), the buttons' pour (one small simulation, see Buttons), the «Разделы» roll (see «Разделы: the roll»), and small state transitions (disclosures, the city pill, row reorder). Nothing else: no split-text reveals, no scrubbed parallax, no typing, no idle loops (the pour's loop stops the frame a pour is out of sight), no counters, no rotation.
- **Text must be visible without JS and at first paint.** Entrances use `gsap.from` inside `useGSAP` after mount, with `clearProps`. Never hide content with CSS waiting for JS.
- Respect motion mode with `useMotionMode()` from `@/lib/motion` (`"full" | "reduced"`): return early unless it is `"full"`, and pass it as a `useGSAP` dependency with `revertOnUpdate: true`. `html[data-motion="reduced"]` also cuts CSS transitions to 1ms.
- No ScrollTrigger pins: use CSS `position: sticky` and let ScrollTrigger only read progress, with triggers on non-sticky parents.
- Never an overflow clip (not even `overflow-x: clip`) on `body` or on anything that holds a sticky element: iOS Safari gives the sticky element a clipping layer, moves it with the page and snaps it back (the phones' coffee band twitched). `html` keeps the page's sideways guard (on the root the clip goes to the viewport); a block that spills sideways clips itself and holds nothing sticky (`.ab-sign`, `.ab-welcome`, `.rv`).
- Motion (framer) uses `m.*` from `motion/react`, never `motion.*` (the app runs `<LazyMotion strict>`). It is only for React-state layout (the city pill, presence). Never let GSAP and Motion animate the same element.
- Hover styles only under `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`. Script-driven hover (the pour) checks each event instead: `pointerType` mouse, or pen with `buttons === 0`; never touch.
- react-hooks v7 lint: no `Math.random()` or `Date.now()` in render (use `mulberry32` from `@/lib/seed`); never read or write `ref.current` during render.
- Four are declared exceptions to these rules, each on its own terms: the coffee poster (the barista, the steam, a cup that reacts to the scroll), the weekly puzzle (pieces the guest moves, and the café's answers), the runner (a loop and a score) and the cafés plan (a red that follows the reading, the guest's dotted way). So are the feedback page's note (pen loops, and the sheet torn off and carried to the café's side) and the about page's hanging sign (hung on a fresh load, then a card on a cord that the guest's hand and the page's scroll move), and so is the menu (the notice turned over, and the table that serves the dish being read). See their sections below. The «Разделы» dialog is chrome, not a section, and moves on its own terms too: see «Разделы: the roll». ## The runner («Игра со стаканчиком»)
A takeaway cup runs through the café: the game section after the evening (ink, `#igra`) and a side block under the 404's head (cream). It is a declared exception to the motion rules (the other is the coffee poster), on these terms:
- **It moves only when the guest starts it.** Before that the stage is a static server-rendered poster (`RunnerPoster`, inline SVG). The loop runs only while a run is in play and stops on pause, when the stage is mostly off screen, on blur and in a hidden tab. It never resumes on its own.
- **Reduced motion** drops the leg cycle and the tab's flutter and makes the start scroll instant. The run itself (the world moving, the jumps) is the game, so it stays, at the same speeds.
- **Touch:** on a touch screen (`pointer: coarse`) the thumbs play on two pads under the field, «Пригнуться» (held) on the left and «Прыжок» on the right, and the note and the buttons sit above the field in one 48px row that never changes height, out of the thumbs' way. The field itself jumps wherever it is pressed (no duck zone). Outside a run «Прыжок» starts, continues or plays again, like the field. A held pad fills at once (no pour). The pads are `aria-hidden`: they double the stage's keys.
- **Pausing by itself:** a press never pauses the game unless it lands on something else to use. Presses in the game (its note, its gaps, the pads) and a finger's near miss off it don't; a link, a button or a field elsewhere does, and so does any mouse click off the game. The keyboard leaving the game, a blur, a hidden tab and the stage going mostly off screen pause it too.
- **Canvas rules:** no text on the canvas (the score and the note are DOM), colours read from `--fg`, `--bg` and `--accent` so it works on either theme, a transparent canvas so the grain shows, the pixel ratio capped at 2, and every listener, observer and frame released on unmount.
- **Colour:** the cup's tomato sleeve is the game's one highlight; the tab is drawn in the field colours, not milk.
- **Theme sequence:** evening, chess, game and footer are all ink; ink after ink draws its gutter hairline by hand (`.chess`, `.game`, `.ft`).
- **Loading:** the island (`RunnerIsland`) is thin; the controller, the drawing and the engine load with `import()` when the game comes near, and must stay type-only imports in the island.
- **The page's chrome:** `html[data-game]` hides the mobile action bar while a run is on screen, and on a touch screen whenever the pads are on screen (the bar would cover them; on a phone on its side it always would).
- **Storage:** the record is `localStorage["kd-runner"] = {"v":1,"best":n}`, validated on read (`src/lib/runner-best.ts`).
- **Tests:** the engine is pure and seeded; `src/lib/runner.test.ts` plays every change with bots across 120 seeds and must stay at zero crashes.

## «До краёв»: the loader
Every page's first load pours, and so does every page switch that has something to load: the screen is one of the site's buttons at full size, 38% full of its ink when the show starts and poured to the brim as the page loads. A declared exception to the motion rules, on these terms:
- **Files:** `assets-src/loader/runtime.js` (the shows, the milestones, the honest needle, the guest's input, the clean finish), `assets-src/loader/pour.js` (the page's side: the cover, the pool, the canvas, the worker's handshake), and the liquid: `liquid-sim.js` (the physics), `liquid-gl.js` (the drawing, in WebGL2) and `liquid-worker.js` (the worker's loop). `node scripts/build-loader.mjs` minifies them into `src/components/loader/loader-code.ts` (generated: never edit it by hand, rebuild it; the three liquid files become one string inside it). `loader-script.ts` fills in the site's own pages (from `content/routes.ts`) and the base path, and the root layout inlines it in `<head>` right after `HEAD_SCRIPT`. `useLoaderSwitch` in `Providers.tsx` lends it the router and reports each page that arrives. Styles: `src/styles/loader.css`. Tests: `src/components/loader/liquid.test.ts` drives the physics. Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-29-loader-liquid-design.md`.
- **When, a load:** the head script decides (`html.ld-on`, `data-ld="full|brisk|calm"`): a fresh load or a reload at the top of any route, seen (not a background tab), with no Save-Data; never on back or forward or a bfcache restore. A later load this session is brisk (`kd-intro`); reduced motion is calm: no liquid, the cream cover simply fades. Its storage is the policy's (`kd-intro`, `kd-scroll`).
- **When, a switch:** a plain click (no modifier, no new tab, no download) on a link to one of the site's own pages that hasn't been shown in this visit; a page already seen has nothing left to load and switches plainly, and so does back or forward. Never with Save-Data or in a background tab, and never for a link out of this build (the other language's site). It is brisk, or calm. The page being left dissolves into the cream cover and its pool (260ms, 200 calm, without the pool), the route is pushed once the cover is up (at most 400ms from the click), and the pour runs on the new page's loading: the route in, its faces, its first screen's images decoded. The router's own scroll and focus happen under the cover. A link in the «Разделы» dialog works the same: the sheet rolls up over the cover as it comes in.
- **The pour:** the cover is the page's cream and grain, nothing else: no title, no numbers, no bar, no words of its own. The liquid is one flat ink with the dark grain: no second tone, no wash, no highlight. The bottom 38% is already poured at the first paint. The realism is in how it moves, not in how it is drawn: the stream falls in from the top edge (from a lip 5 cm above the screen, at 0.26 mm to the CSS px), thinning as it speeds up, arcing a little (it leaves the spout moving sideways) and coming in gulps while its pour point drifts slowly; it plunges in, digs a crater that rings, and throws up droplets that fly and fall back, joining the surface as they go in. The surface is the buttons' liquid (`pour.ts`) at the screen's scale: the tank's own sloshing modes, spilling where a face gets too steep and climbing the walls a little. It is one pour from the first drop to the brim: when the needle waits, the stream thins to a thread (easing there and back) and never stops. The level is the progress and nothing else: it rises only as the page really loads (a load: the DOM, the faces, the first screen's images, hydration, window load), creeps at most 60% into the largest share still out and never goes back. At the brim the glass drains toward the left wall (the outlet opens over its first 0.16s, 0.1 brisk, then Torricelli: the surface comes in over the top edge gently, runs, and slows into the floor) and hands over the live page.
- **It never has to load:** everything is inline in `<head>`. The worker is made from a Blob of the inlined code, the ink's grain is drawn in the worker, and nothing new is fetched (the page's pool and the cover use the site's own grain textures, as before). The first paint is the cover and the pool as plain HTML. The worker's GPU context and shaders can take a moment while the page itself loads (the first WebGL context of a visit, a GPU busy painting the page), so until the worker draws, the page's pool waits, still, where it first painted (nothing moved from the main thread can move smoothly while a page loads: parsing and hydration give it a frame now and then, and a pool raised there rose in hops), and the worker's glass takes it up there with the stream falling in, then catches up with the needle. The page's pool stays under the canvas until the cover goes at the brim: the canvas's first frames can reach the screen a frame after they are drawn, and the surface never dips under where the pour started. The shaders are built on the driver's own threads (`KHR_parallel_shader_compile`), so nothing waits on them. A worker that is not drawing yet when the page is ready is never waited for: the cover fades.
- **Smooth through the page's own work:** the physics is stepped and drawn in the Web Worker, on its own `requestAnimationFrame`, onto an OffscreenCanvas, so hydration or a route rendering on a slow phone never holds a frame up (tested: the main thread blocked for 600ms, and the worker's frames kept coming every 16.7ms). The main thread only passes on the milestones (and the needle, until the pour starts) and the exit's handshake. The worker steps the needle itself, on its own clock, from the same milestones by the runtime's own rule, so a main thread held up by hydration never makes the level wait and then rush (the runtime's clock gets a frame now and then there, and a needle read from it stands still and leaps). The exit's handshake: the worker says `brim` (the cover goes, unseen under the ink, and the worker is told `go`), `drain` and `done`. The canvas is capped at 2 device pixels to the CSS px and about 4 million pixels. The page's own animations and its pointer are handed back when the drain starts (`ctx.uncover()`, `html.ld-reveal`).
- **Without a worker or WebGL2** (no OffscreenCanvas, a context or a shader that fails, a lost context): the page's pool is the whole pour, rising with the needle, and the cover fades at the end.
- **Never in React's way:** the root is a `<ld-screen>` custom element under `<html>`, made before `<body>` exists on a load (React hydrates `<html>` and `<body>` by tag name and skips a tag it doesn't expect; a `div` would be taken for Next's own), and it is removed at the end with every class it added, then `kd:loader-done` fires (`detail.kind`: `load` or `nav`). The page paints under it the whole time (its LCP included); its CSS animations wait under the cover and play from the drain (the hero's first sway, the about page's opening).
- **The guest is never held:** the page doesn't scroll under the cover (unless a load has a hash to land on), asking to move on (a wheel, a swipe, a scroll key, a tap) hurries it, and Tab ends it at once (on a load, focus landing anywhere does too; on a switch the router moves the focus itself). A switch cut short still goes where the guest asked. A load goes after 7s of visible time (5 brisk) whatever is missing; the head script ends it at DOMContentLoaded if it never started and at 15s if its first frame never came. A switch goes after 5s once its page is in, and after 12s whatever happens.

- **When:** the head script sets `html[data-season]` before first paint from the Moscow date: `eve` (20 November to 31 December), `ny` (1 to 6 and 8 to 12 January), `xmas` (7 January), `old` (13 and 14 January); outside the window nothing is set and nothing seasonal shows (CSS hides it all, the islands don't load their code). `SeasonIsland` turns the phase over at Moscow midnight on an open page. `?season=eve|ny|xmas|old|off` previews a phase on any date (never stored). Change the dates in `SEASON` only: `season.test.ts` checks the head script against `seasonPhase()` for every day of a year.
- **The line:** the hero's reply ends with the phase's greeting (`SeasonGreeting`, `t.season.greet`): «С наступающим!», «С Новым годом!», «С Рождеством!», «Со старым Новым годом!» («Happy holidays!», «Happy New Year!», «Merry Christmas!», «Happy Old New Year!»). All four are on the page and CSS shows one. It is part of the reply, so a screen reader hears it there. No other copy changes: no invented dishes, prices or hours.
- **Colour:** the site's own. The garland's wire and nails are the ink, its glass the tomato and the milk (the season is the one time red is more than one small highlight); the snow is the milk, so it shows the way snow shows: hardly at all against the day's cream, white over a photo, thick over the evening's ink and the coffee poster's red. The snowflakes are the notice's paper (`--paper-tex` on `--paper`) with a cut edge's hairline and a short paper shadow. No glow, no gradient, no second tone.
- **The garland** (`Garland`, `garland-controller.ts`; at the top of every page, in the root layout, so it stays lit from page to page): hung on nails under the header's line, gutter to gutter, in its own box above the sections and under the header, which covers it as the page scrolls on. Its bulbs are the song's notes in order, half a verse (14 bulbs) to as much of it as brings their pitch nearest 2.8rem, a nail every four. The physics sees a real garland whatever its size (bulbs 12 cm apart): an inextensible cord of nodes pinned on the nails, each bulb a pendulum on its socket's lead, position-based dynamics in fixed steps of 1/1200 s. The page's acceleration acts on it (as on the about page's sign: a fling presses it down, a hard stop lets it jump; a jump of the page, an anchor or a new page, is no acceleration). A cursor brushes the bulbs with a soft contact (a quick pass nudges, a slow one carries); a mouse or a pen can take a bulb, carry it up to 7 cm and let it swing (past 13 cm it slips out of the fingers); a click or a tap knocks it; touch never holds one, so the page keeps its scroll. The frame loop runs only while something moves and never while the garland is off screen. The lights come on in a wave from the first bulb, by CSS, held under the loader like every animation; a garland hung again at a new width is simply lit. At midnight on 1 January every light blinks at each stroke of the chimes.
- **Paper snowflakes** (`PaperFlake`, server-rendered): cut by `cutFlake(seed)` as children cut them, a sheet folded in twelve: a spine down each arm, branches at sixty degrees, a plate at the heart, sometimes a ring or a jewel; holes only inside a plate or a broad arm. The cutter unfolds its work and sews back any hole that let paper loose, so a flake is always one piece (`flake.test.ts` checks a hundred, and the site's own at twice the grid). Each is an SVG mask in a CSS custom property over the notice's paper, so it is there at first paint and without the bundle. Where they are: the hero's notice (two corners) and its photo's corner; on `/menu` the hero's corner flake shows from behind the bottom corner of the notice turned over (it is stuck on the front); the plan's sheet on `/locations`; the pad's letterhead on `/feedback`; the about page's sign, where it swings and turns with the card (and takes no presses: the sign does). Seeds in `src/content/season.ts`, places in `season.css` (in the paper's own width, so they keep their place at every size).
- **Peeling** (`FlakePeel`, full motion only): a press lifts a flake (it gives toward the hand, its shadow lengthening); pulled 30px, or clicked, it comes off and falls in a layer of its own as a fluttering plate (`fallStep`: Andersen, Pesavento and Wang's quasi-steady model, in a vertical plane through the guest's line of sight, so it rocks toward them and away and its face flashes), until it is below the screen. It stays off until the page is opened again; a touch on a flake doesn't scroll the page.
- **Snow** (`Snowfall`, `snow-controller.ts`): a fixed canvas under the header and the action bar, capped at 1.5 device pixels to the CSS px and at about one flake per 7600 px² (240 at most). A flake takes the air's velocity within a quarter of a second, plus its fall and its own zigzag; the air is a gusting mean wind, three slow eddies from a stream function (divergence-free, tested), the drag of the scrolling page (the far, small flakes next to it are carried most) and the wake of a moving cursor. It starts once the loader is gone and sleeps under any cover (the loader, «Разделы», a run of the game) and in a hidden tab. Reduced motion and Save-Data: no fall.
- **Snow that settles** (the footer's wordmark, on every page): a canvas laid over the letters, moving with the page. Its grid comes from the wordmark's own shape; a far flake that comes down onto a letter or onto snow rests there and slides while a lower cell is free, never on a slope steeper than snow's angle of repose (about 45°, looked at four cells across) or on a hairline, and no deeper than six cells: so caps grow on the flat tops, the balls and the clover's leaves and never cling to the slanting strokes. The evening's snow is already there when the guest comes, in every motion mode.
- **Sound** (`SoundToggle`, `sound.ts`): a bell in the header's end, before the language, only in the season and with scripts. Off until it is pressed (a browser lets a page sound only after a press anyway), remembered only for the visit, never stored. Everything is synthesized with Web Audio, nothing is fetched: the bulbs are glockenspiel bars (the inharmonic partials of a free bar) an octave up; turning it on shakes sleigh bells and plays the verse along the garland, every note knocking its bulb; a torn tab rips (crackling fibres in a band of noise); a peeled flake peels; at midnight on 1 January, Moscow time, the Kremlin clock strikes twelve (a big bell's partials, detuned so they beat), every 3.2 s, and on the twelfth the line becomes «С Новым годом!» (the strokes and the blinking garland come with or without the sound). Voices are capped so a hand dragged through the garland never roars; a hidden tab suspends it all; on an iPhone the silent switch is overridden, since the guest asked.
- **Without JS** nothing seasonal shows (the head script decides); with the head script but an old browser, the snowflakes and the line show and nothing moves. **Forced colours and print** drop the garland, the snow and the snowflakes.
- **Verification:** `?season=…` to see a phase; `node scripts/shot.mjs` without `--reduced` and with `--wait=6000` to see the lights on and the snow (the loader goes first); the midnight needs Playwright's `page.clock` (install it at 23:59:30 Moscow time on 31 December and run it forward).

## Accessibility
- One `h1` per page (the hero on `/`, the title on `/menu` and on `/locations` via `DialogueHeading as="h1"`), an `h2` per section via `DialogueHeading`, real lists and landmarks. Alt text comes from `photos.ts`.
- Visible focus is global: a 2px `--focus` ring with a 3px offset. Never remove it; inside anything that clips, or on the screen edge, use a negative `outline-offset`.
- Touch targets are at least 44px. Decorative SVG is `aria-hidden`.
- External links get `target="_blank" rel="noreferrer"` and the sr-only «(откроется в новой вкладке)».
- The page works without JS: every block renders on the server, the city prices fall back to Нальчик, the hero's checked radio picks the panel, and the reviews' pile shows its first note (its arrows wait for JS).
- One exception: without JS the runner can't start, so its block is hidden (`html:not(.js)`), like the header's «Разделы».
- `html.js` means the site's scripts will run: the head script sets it only when `PROBE_SCRIPT` (its own inline `<script>`, before it) parses and runs, which takes the newest syntax and built-ins the bundle ships (Safari 15.4 and up). A browser too old for the bundle keeps the no-JS page, with no loader and no intro, never a page set for scripts that don't run. Keep the probe in step with the `browserslist`.
