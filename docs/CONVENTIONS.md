# «Как дела?»: frontend conventions

The site reads like a well-set printed café menu: warm paper, black ink, one red. The character comes from four things only: the real logo with its red clover, heavy condensed display type, real food photography, and one interaction (the hero's tear-off notice). Everything else stays quiet, aligned and the same from section to section. A section has at most one signature moment. When in doubt, remove it. Three sections are declared exceptions, each on the terms of its own section below: the coffee poster, the weekly puzzle and the runner; so is the feedback page's note (see «Обратная связь»).

## Stack
- Next.js 16 App Router, React 19.3, TypeScript, Tailwind v4 (CSS-first), GSAP 3.15 with `@gsap/react`, Motion 13, Lenis.
- Read `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` before using a Next API. In Next 16:
  - `next/image` uses `preload`, not `priority`;
  - `images.qualities` is `[75, 85]`.

## Files and ownership
- `src/app/page.tsx` composes the page: hero, team, breakfasts, coffee, locations, reviews, evening, chess, game inside `<main>`, then the footer (the contentinfo landmark).
- `src/app/feedback/page.tsx` is «Обратная связь»: its heading and the note (`src/components/feedback/*`), then «Уже спрашивали.» and the footer; see «Обратная связь» below.
- The runner lives in `src/components/game/*` (`Runner`, used by the game section and the 404) with its pure engine in `src/lib/runner.ts`; see «The runner» below.
- The weekly puzzle's rules are `src/lib/chess.ts`, its site logic (weeks, grades, Russian, the saved solve) `src/lib/puzzle.ts`, its puzzles `src/content/puzzles.ts`; see «The weekly puzzle» below.
- Each section:
  - is `src/components/sections/<Name>.tsx`, a **server component** that renders all copy;
  - may add client islands next to it as `src/components/sections/<name>/<Island>.tsx` (motion, measurement, state);
  - owns its own stylesheet, `src/styles/sections/<name>.css`. It is already imported by `globals.css`.
- Shared styles: `src/app/globals.css` (tokens, themes, type, heading, buttons, links, rules, menu row), `src/styles/brand.css` (Photo, Slip), `src/styles/chrome.css` (header, dialog, action bar, city switch, status), `src/styles/hero.css`.
- Content is typed data in `src/content/*`: `answers`, `menu`, `cafes`, `people`, `photos`, `routes` and `puzzles` (generated by `npm run assets:puzzles`, never edited by hand). UI strings are in `src/i18n/ru.ts` as `t.*`. Don't hard-code data that exists there, and never invent prices, hours, phones, addresses or ratings. Only add a string to `ru.ts` if there is no suitable one.
- Brand components live in `src/components/brand/*`: `Wordmark` (full | compact | badge; the only place the octagon and the clover appear, outside the coffee poster), `Photo` and `Slip` (the paper slip, below). The coffee poster's own art (the cup, the hatched clover, the octagon photo) lives with it in `src/components/sections/coffee/`.
- Chrome components live in `src/components/chrome/*`: `RouteLink` (**always** use it for internal links, never raw hrefs; `hash` adds an anchor: `<RouteLink to="menu" hash="shakshuka">`), `BtnLabel` (the contents of every `.btn`), `CitySwitch`, `CafeStatus`, and `pour-controller.ts` (the buttons' pour, started by `Providers`).
- `DialogueHeading` is in `src/components/type/DialogueHeading.tsx`. Every section heading uses it.

## Design tokens (globals.css)
- **Colours:** `cream` (page), `milk` (the rare raised surface: the hero notice, the torn tab that lands in its answer, the team's slips, the reviews' 5,0 slip and notes, and the puzzle's grade), `ink` (text, and the dark sections' background), `graphite` (secondary text on cream), `tomato` / `tomato-ink` (the one accent). Tomato is for the logo clover, focus and at most one small highlight per viewport. It is never a section background (except the coffee poster) and never body text. No other colours (the coffee poster adds its cup's cobalt, scoped to `.coffee`).
- **Themes:** `data-theme="cream"` or `data-theme="ink"` on `section.field` (and `"tomato"` for the coffee poster alone). Hero through reviews are cream, except the coffee poster; evening, chess, game and footer are ink (the page is one café day and ends at night). Never put `data-theme` on anything inside a section: the header and the action bar take the last themed element under their line. Each theme sets `--bg`, `--fg`, `--fg-soft`, `--accent`, `--accent-ink` (red for small text, lifted on ink), `--focus` and `--grain`. `.field` paints them; `.field::before` is the grain, `::after` is free. Two cream sections in a row get a gutter-to-gutter hairline automatically, so don't add a top border. The header, the action bar and the menu dialog take the theme of the section beneath them.
- **Type sizes:** `--fs-body`, `--fs-small` (.875rem), `--fs-label` (.8125rem), `--lead`, `--h2` (the one section-title size), `--h3` (item titles). No per-section poster sizes, except the coffee answer.
- **Spacing:** `.section-pad` (`--section-pad`, bottom 1.15× the top), `--head-gap` (heading block → content), `--foot-gap` (a list or grid → the closing line under it: links, a note, the pie; the same step in every section), `--gutter`, `--grid-gap`, `--header-h`.
- **Lines:** `--line` (16% of the text colour), `--line-strong` (28%), `--rule` (1px). Use `.rule-t` / `.rule-b`. No heavier rules (the coffee card's 1.5px ink border is the exception).
- **Radius:** 2px for photos and panels, 999px for buttons and toggles only.
- **Shadows:** none, except the paper shadow (`--paper-shadow`) under the hero notice, its landed tab, the team's slips, the reviews' 5,0 slip and notes, the puzzle's grade, and the coffee poster's flat offset shadows (the cup, the card).
- **Paper:** `--paper`, `--paper-tex`, `--paper-shadow` and `--edge` are global tokens: the hero notice, its torn strips, the team's slips and the reviews' slip and notes are one paper.
- **Layout:** every section is full viewport width. `.wrap` has no max width; it only adds `--gutter` (16px on phones, `clamp(24px, 3.4vw, 104px)` from 640px). Desktop is a 12-column grid from 1024px, gutter to gutter, with `--grid-gap` column gaps. Compose for 390, 1440, 1920 and 2560 alike: no centred column, no dead halves, never horizontal scroll. The one centred composition is «Отзывы»: its pile of notes on columns 3 to 10, the arrows centred under it. Running text keeps a measure (≤ 60ch; `.lead` 44ch). `.bleed`, `.bleed-l` and `.bleed-r` break imagery out to the screen edge; text always keeps the gutter. Past 1600px the root font size grows (16px → 20px at 2560px), so anything in rem scales with the canvas.

## Type
Two families, plus the barista's hand in one place:
- **Display** (Sofia Sans Extra Condensed): `.display`, heavy (860) uppercase, line-height .9. Section titles, dish names, big numbers. Pair `.item-title` (`--h3`) with it for dish and item names.
- **Sans** (Montserrat, the café's own face): body, UI, buttons, labels, prices, hours, phones.
  - `.mono` is Montserrat with tabular figures, for prices, hours and phones. **₽ is never set in the display face.**
  - `.label` is small Montserrat 500 in `--fg-soft`, sentence case, no uppercase, no tracking.
  - `.lead` is the section lead: sans at `--lead`, `--fg-soft`, 44ch, `text-wrap: balance` (two to four even lines, usually broken at the dash).
  - `.reply` is the café's voice: Montserrat italic (400; running text is 450, `--fw-text`), only for the team statement, the review quotes, the hero's reply line, the coffee poster's reply and the reply under the puzzle's grade. At most one italic moment per section; never for captions, labels, buttons or notes. No serif anywhere.
- **Hand** (Neucha, `--font-hand`, not preloaded): only the barista's notes in the coffee poster. Nowhere else.

Headings get `text-wrap: balance`, paragraphs `pretty`. Keep «ёлочки».

## Section heading
```tsx
<header className="section-head">
  <DialogueHeading id="zavtraki-title" q="Завтраки" a="Что на завтрак?" />
  <p className="lead">…</p>
</header>
```
- `q` is the section's plain name (not shown; it is the title when there is no `a` and no children); `a` is the title in display type at `--h2`. Other props: `as` (`h2` | `h3`), `id`, `className`, `answerClassName` (on the title), `children` (after the title, inside the heading), and `ask`: a question line over the title in the thin display voice (`.dlg-ask`), used only by the coffee poster («А кофе?»), so its heading reads «А кофе? А с кофе мы не шутим.».
- It renders `h2.dlg > [span.dlg-ask] + span.dlg-title.display`. `data-dlg-q` (the `ask` line) and `data-dlg-a` (the title) are motion hooks.
- The label is `--fs-small` up to a laptop and then grows with the title (about 1:6.6 from 1440 to 2560), so it doesn't get lost under a 150px title.
- From 1024px the title spans columns 1–8 and the lead sits in 9–12 on the title's last baseline; phones stack. `--head-gap` below is included.
- One section passes children instead of a title: Team (its italic statement). «Отзывы» asks in its title («Как вам у нас?») and the flagship's 5,0 answers on a slip beside it, on the title's line (`.rv-head`; the slip goes under the title where the line is too short). The slip is only paper to a screen reader, so an `.sr-only` line after the heading says whose score it is.
- **No em dashes, anywhere**: not in copy, alt text, titles, screen reader text, comments or docs, and not as dialogue marks (the hero's exchange is «Как дела?» / «Сонно.» with no dash). Use a comma, a colon, a full stop or «·» instead, or rephrase.

## Components
- **Photo:** `<Photo photo={p} sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 33vw, 100vw" aspect="4/5" />`, also `className`, `preload` (the one LCP image only) and `loading`.
  - Rectangular, 2px radius, `object-fit: cover`, blur placeholder. No frames, no octagons (the coffee card's `OctPhoto` is the one exception).
  - `aspect` is `"w/h"` (default `"4/5"`) and sets `--photo-aspect`. Keep it true to the rendered shape; a section may override `aspect-ratio` in CSS or set it to `auto` with a height.
  - `sizes` describes the frame's width; `coverSizes` scales it when the source is wider than the frame.
  - Stock stand-ins (`placeholder: true` in `photos.ts`) get a quiet «Фото временное» badge, hidden from screen readers (the alt text already says so). Add `photo--sm` on thumbnails about 96–128px wide for a smaller badge.
- **Buttons:** `<a className="btn btn-primary"><BtnLabel>Смотреть меню</BtnLabel></a>` and `<a className="btn"><BtnLabel>…</BtnLabel></a>`. Pill, 48px tall, Montserrat 500. The primary is filled with the text colour and inverts on ink; the secondary has a `--line-strong` border. At most two in a group; prefer one button plus text links.
  - **The pour:** hover or a press (a mouse, or a pen; never touch) or keyboard focus fills the pill from the bottom with one flat liquid: the text colour into the secondary (ink on cream, milk on ink), tomato ink into the primary (milk words). No second tone, wash, highlight or gradient: the realism is all in the surface's shape and motion. The liquid is simulated (`src/lib/pour.ts`, tested in `pour.test.ts`): the pill is a 4 cm tank, poured into down the end wall on the pointer's side (keyboard: the end the focus came from) with a gush that eases off toward the brim, the level following its real cross-section. The surface is the tank's own sloshing modes at water's tempo for that depth: the poured wedge runs across the floor and back, and the pill tops up unevenly. The drain runs out at the exit-side wall with a falling head. About 0.75s in, 0.5s out, reversible at any moment. `BtnLabel`'s copy of the label sits inside `.btn__pour` and is clipped with it, so the words turn over exactly at the surface. A press sinks the pill to 0.965 and springs it back; it never drains it.
  - `pour-controller.ts` runs every pour: listeners delegated from `document`, one frame loop that runs only while a pour moves and stops the frame it is out of sight, and the only writer of `.btn__pour`'s inline `clip-path` (`none` brim-full, removed empty). Never give `.btn__pour` a React `style`, and never toggle a `.btn`'s classes from script (React owns them: the runner swaps `btn-primary`). Never promote `.btn__pour` to a layer of its own (`will-change`, a transform, opacity, a filter): its copy would raster apart from the real label and change weight at the surface. On hover the filled pill keeps its own edge (only the outline pill's border turns to the text colour).
  - Put everything visible inside `BtnLabel` (the chess CTA's «↗» too) and keep `.sr-only` text outside it: the copy is `aria-hidden`. Forced colours drop the pour. Whenever the simulation isn't running (no JS, before hydration, reduced motion) CSS fills the pill with a flat level instead (`html:not([data-pour])`), and reduced motion makes that an instant swap.
- **Text links:** `<a className="dlink">Все кофейни</a>`. Montserrat 500 at the surrounding size, a 1px underline at 35% that turns solid on hover (fine pointers) or press, and a 44px hit area on touch.
  - A group of links (a section's closing pair) goes in `<p className="dlinks">`: one line when they fit, otherwise stacked; on touch the rows are 44px apart, so the hit areas never overlap.
- **External links:**
  ```tsx
  <a className="dlink" href={…} target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">
    Telegram<span className="dlink__ext" aria-hidden="true">↗</span>
    <span className="sr-only"> {t.a11y.newTab}</span>
  </a>
  ```
- **Tags and features:** plain text joined by « · » in `.label`. No pill chips.
- **Menu rows:** name and price, no dotted leader (the coffee card is the exception: see «Coffee: the poster»).
  - Primary rows (breakfasts, desserts): the name in `.display.item-title`, the price in `.mono`, an optional description line below. Stacked lists (breakfasts) separate rows with 1px `--line` rules, make every row as tall as the tallest (`grid-auto-rows: 1fr` on the list) and set the price in a column of its own, centred vertically in the row beside the name and description.
  - Secondary rows in sans (the evening pie) use `.menu-row` from globals.css: the name may wrap and the price stands on its last line, at least 1.5rem from it (the hero's reply is the exception: its price stands centred beside the reply's lines):
    ```tsx
    <p className="menu-row">
      <span className="menu-row__name">{name}</span>
      <span className="menu-row__price mono">{rub(350)}</span>
    </p>
    ```
  - A city without a price shows `t.hero.askLater` («уточняйте в кофейне») instead.
- **Status:** `<CafeStatus cafe={c} />` renders `.status` as words only («открыто · до 23:00»): no dot, no green, no pulse.
- **City switch:** `<CitySwitch compact? labelledBy? />` is the segmented pill toggle. It is named «Город» unless `labelledBy` points at a visible label.
- **Clover:** only inside the logo (`Wordmark`). No clover bullets or decorations. The coffee poster is the exception: the cup's decal and the hatched clover behind it.
- **Paper slip:** a strip of the notice's paper with one word or number in display type, tilted (`--tilt`), with a torn end (`--tear`) and a lighter fringe along it (`--fibres`), all from `tornStrip(id)` in `src/lib/tear.ts`. Four exist, and all render through `<Slip seed label mood? className />` (`src/components/brand/Slip.tsx`, look in brand.css; the caller's class only places and sizes it): the tab that lands in the hero's answer, the team's answers (one on the foot of each portrait, each with its tab's tear), the reviews' 5,0 beside its title, and the puzzle's grade (the same answer's tab). Its tilt lives in CSS `rotate`, never in a tweened `transform`: GSAP folds it into its transform during a landing and hands it back on `clearProps`.
- **Pile (reviews only):** the guests' words on notes of the notice's paper, torn off a pad along the top (`tornSheet(id)` in `src/lib/tear.ts`: the rip, its fringe and a small lean of the note's own), lying in a loose pile.
  - Every note is server-rendered and stacked in one grid cell, so the longest quote sets the pile's size. Where a note lies is its `data-pos` (0 on top) as CSS `translate` and `rotate` on the `li`: the server sets them in order and `ReviewsPile` moves them. Only the top note's words are shown, and read.
  - Next throws the top note aside and it slides back in under the pile while the others move up a place: the → pill, a click or a tap on the note, or a sideways drag let go past 90px or flicked (a mouse, a pen or a finger; up and down stays the page's scroll). While it is dragged, the note under it starts to come up. Back (the ← pill) pulls the bottom note out from under the pile and lays it on top. It loops, and it never moves on its own.
  - One motion per wrapper: the `li`'s place is CSS, the landing is `ReviewsMotion`'s on `.rv-note__land`, the throw and the drag are `ReviewsPile`'s on `.rv-note__fly`.
  - The pills are `.btn`s with `aria-controls`, and a polite live region reads «Отзыв 2 из 3: …» on each change. No dots: without JS the first note stays on top and the pills keep their place, unseen.

## Formatting and content
- Prices go through `rub()` from `src/lib/format.ts` (a no-break space and ₽). Counts go through `plural()` (via a `t.*` function in `ru.ts`), digit groups through `groupDigits()`.
- Keep copy plain and pass it through `typograf()` from `src/lib/typograf.ts` where it renders (no-break spaces after short words, before «·», in numbers and units, in addresses, and inside the brand's «как дела?»). `bindLastWord()` keeps a paragraph from ending on a lone word; `keepWhole()` keeps names whole (no break at a hyphen, none inside a short «…» name) for dish names, answers and quotes. `quote()` sets a guest's words in «…», with a name inside turned to „…“; it goes last: `quote(bindLastWord(keepWhole(typograf(text))))`. Take `NBSP` / `WJ` from there; don't write a local helper.
- Digit groups use a plain no-break space («2 709», «5 000 ₽»). Montserrat's narrow no-break space (U+202F) is too thin to see at label size, so don't use it.
- **City-specific content:** render **both cities on the server** and wrap each in `data-city-only="nalchik|vladikavkaz"`. CSS hides the inactive one (from `html[data-city]`, set before paint), so switching city never re-renders. `useKd((s) => s.city)` is only for things that must compute.
- Copy is plain, specific and warm, in the «вы» register. Keep the brand's dish names. No meta-instructions, asides, sign-offs or cute microcopy.

## Motion rules
- Client code imports GSAP from `@/lib/gsap`: `gsap`, `ScrollTrigger` and `useGSAP`, with the eases `kd.out`, `kd.inOut`, `kd.peel` and `kd.settle` registered. For the coffee poster it also registers `DrawSVGPlugin`.
- Allowed: one quiet entrance per block (opacity plus y 16–24px, `kd.out`, 0.6–0.8s, stagger ≤ 0.06, once), the hero's tear-off and its answer change, the breakfasts photo crossfade, the reviews' pile (the top note thrown aside, tilting as it goes, in 0.42s and back in under the pile in 0.6s, the others moving up a place in 0.56s, a dragged note following the pointer) and the one-time landing of its 5,0 slip and its notes (the hero's landing, the bottom note first), the team's slips landing on their portraits as each row rises (the hero's landing: y -26, scale 1.05, `kd.settle`), the buttons' pour (one small simulation, see Buttons), and small state transitions (disclosures, the city pill, row reorder). Nothing else: no split-text reveals, no scrubbed parallax, no typing, no idle loops (the pour's loop stops the frame a pour is out of sight), no counters, no rotation.
- **Text must be visible without JS and at first paint.** Entrances use `gsap.from` inside `useGSAP` after mount, with `clearProps`. Never hide content with CSS waiting for JS.
- Respect motion mode with `useMotionMode()` from `@/lib/motion` (`"full" | "reduced"`): return early unless it is `"full"`, and pass it as a `useGSAP` dependency with `revertOnUpdate: true`. `html[data-motion="reduced"]` also cuts CSS transitions to 1ms.
- No ScrollTrigger pins: use CSS `position: sticky` and let ScrollTrigger only read progress, with triggers on non-sticky parents.
- Motion (framer) uses `m.*` from `motion/react`, never `motion.*` (the app runs `<LazyMotion strict>`). It is only for React-state layout (the city pill, presence). Never let GSAP and Motion animate the same element.
- Hover styles only under `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`. Script-driven hover (the pour) checks each event instead: `pointerType` mouse, or pen with `buttons === 0`; never touch.
- react-hooks v7 lint: no `Math.random()` or `Date.now()` in render (use `mulberry32` from `@/lib/seed`); never read or write `ref.current` during render.
- Three sections are declared exceptions to these rules, each on its own terms: the coffee poster (the barista, the steam, a cup that reacts to the scroll), the weekly puzzle (pieces the guest moves, and the café's answers) and the runner (a loop and a score). So is the feedback page's note (pen loops, and the sheet torn off and carried to the café's side). See their sections below.

## Coffee: the poster («А кофе?», `#kofe`)
The coffee section is the page's one poster: in the middle of the café day it says «А с кофе мы не шутим.» out loud. It is the only place the quiet rules above relax, and only in the ways listed here; nothing from it travels to another section.
- **Files:** `sections/Coffee.tsx`, `styles/sections/coffee.css` and `sections/coffee/*`: the art (`Cup` with `CupGround` and `CupSteam`, `HatchedClover`, `OctPhoto`, `NoteSvg`), the note's words and geometry (`note.ts`, tested in `note.test.ts`), the cup as a rocking body (`rock.ts`, tested in `rock.test.ts`), the pen (`pen.ts`), the cup's life (`cup-life.ts`) and the one island, `CoffeeMotion`.
- **Field:** `data-theme="tomato"`: the tomato ground, dark grain at .5, milk text. `--fg-soft`, `--accent`, `--accent-ink` and `--focus` are solid milk (milk on tomato is 4.6:1, and any transparency drops small text below AA), so the header's clover, the phone action bar and every focus ring turn milk over it. Desktop adds a soft poster light.
- **Colour:** cobalt for the cup only, plus the poster's own shades (the halo, the flat shadows, the card's rules), all as custom properties on `.coffee` (`--kofe-*`), never as global tokens.
- **Heading:** `DialogueHeading` with `ask`: «А кофе?» in the thin display voice, then the answer on one line from gutter to gutter (`.coffee__answer`: weight 930, `min(100cqi / 8.69, 13.75rem)` from 1024px). The answer sets 8.40em wide: re-measure it when the copy, the weight or the face changes. Phones: two lines, broken where typograf allows («кофе | мы»). No dialogue dashes.
- **Art:** the cobalt takeaway cup (a milk lid, the white clover decal, a milk sleeve with the note) with a flat shadow and a counter line, in a CSS-sticky column; the logo clover in crayon hatch, tone on tone, behind it. Phones get a small sticky band under the header instead: the cup and the note beside it.
- **Card:** milk paper (`--paper-tex`), a 1.5px ink border, 2px radius and a flat offset shadow. Its ink tokens are custom properties on `.coffee__card`, never a `data-theme`. The head is the site's only tracked caps («01 / Фирменное», «Цены · Нальчик»). Each row: the name (a link to the drink on the bar menu), a dotted leader (`.drink__leader`, only here), the price, the description, the barista's note, and the photo in the logo's octagon with a hairline (`OctPhoto`, only here; without a photo, the logo badge). One column up to 1600px, a two-column board from there.
- **Hand:** Neucha sets the barista's notes and nothing else: on the cup's sleeve, in the phones' band and under each drink. A note is lower case: a question, a drawn arrow (`ARROW_D`), then the drink and its price, built by `noteLines()` from `menu.ts` (`Drink.cupNote` holds the words, never the price).
- **Voice:** the reply beside the cup is `.reply`, the section's one italic moment.
- **Motion** (full motion only), beyond the rules above:
  - the exchange lands: the question from the left, the answer rising out of a clip, then the reply;
  - the cup is one rigid body (`rock.ts`): it rocks on the rim of its base, never through the counter, and each clack onto the other rim keeps only part of its speed, so it settles as a real cup does, each rock smaller and quicker than the last. Everything that moves it is a hand on that body (`CupLife.hold` in `cup-life.ts`): the landing, the pen and a fling's draught. No shakes, no wiggle eases, no tweens on the rock layer;
  - the cup is set down: a hand lowers it tipped onto its right rim while its contact shadow gathers on the counter and the clover draws itself (DrawSVG), lets go, and it rocks onto its base; then the barista writes «как дела?»;
  - each drink that crosses the stuck cup's sleeve (phones: 60% of the screen) gets its note hand-written on the cup, a drawn tomato arrow, a red name and a tilted photo, once the scroll slows there: a fast scroll past several drinks writes only the one it stops on. Pointing at a drink (fine pointers) or tabbing to it writes that one instead; any scroll hands the cup back;
  - the pen inks each line in by drawing a wide wave over it as the line's mask (`penStroke`), so the letters arrive behind a moving nib; it draws the arrow as a stroke, and rubs a note out by drawing the wave back, from wherever the last note got to. Each drink's note sits at its own slant (`noteSlant`). While the pen works it drags the cup onto its right rim; when it lifts, the cup rocks back onto its base. The phones' band inks its HTML lines behind a soft mask edge (`--ink`) and draws the scribble on the small cup;
  - steam loops from the lid, only while the cup is on screen, and streams with the scroll about the sip slot: it leans toward the card, stretches when the page goes down, squats when it goes up, and curls back past upright when the page stops. A hard fling (from 2600 px/s) pulls the cup onto its right rim, toward the card rushing past, and lets it drop back when the fling eases. The clover behind the cup turns a few degrees with the scroll (scrubbed);
  - one motion per element: the landing on the drop, the rocking on the rock, the contact shadow outside both; the wrap itself never moves (the island measures the sleeve from it). Never tween `.coffee__halo` itself (GSAP would bake its CSS translate and rotate into pixels); turn `.coffee__halo-turn`.
- **Reduced motion and no JS** show the finished poster: the cup says «как дела?», every row carries its note, the band doesn't stick, and there is no steam.

## The runner («Игра со стаканчиком»)
A takeaway cup runs through the café: the game section after the evening (ink, `#igra`) and a side block under the 404's head (cream). It is a declared exception to the motion rules (the other is the coffee poster), on these terms:
- **It moves only when the guest starts it.** Before that the stage is a static server-rendered poster (`RunnerPoster`, inline SVG). The loop runs only while a run is in play and stops on pause, when the stage is mostly off screen, on blur and in a hidden tab. It never resumes on its own.
- **Reduced motion** drops the leg cycle and the tab's flutter and makes the start scroll instant. The run itself (the world moving, the jumps) is the game, so it stays, at the same speeds.
- **Canvas rules:** no text on the canvas (the score and the note are DOM), colours read from `--fg`, `--bg` and `--accent` so it works on either theme, a transparent canvas so the grain shows, the pixel ratio capped at 2, and every listener, observer and frame released on unmount.
- **Colour:** the cup's tomato sleeve is the game's one highlight; the tab is drawn in the field colours, not milk.
- **Theme sequence:** evening, chess, game and footer are all ink; ink after ink draws its gutter hairline by hand (`.chess`, `.game`, `.ft`).
- **Loading:** the island (`RunnerIsland`) is thin; the controller, the drawing and the engine load with `import()` when the game comes near, and must stay type-only imports in the island.
- **The page's chrome:** `html[data-game]` hides the mobile action bar while a run is on screen.
- **Storage:** the record is `localStorage["kd-runner"] = {"v":1,"best":n}`, validated on read (`src/lib/runner-best.ts`).
- **Tests:** the engine is pure and seeded; `src/lib/runner.test.ts` plays every change with bots across 120 seeds and must stay at zero crashes.

## The weekly puzzle («Задача недели.», `#shahmaty`)
After the desserts, before the runner: a mate in two from a real game, new every Monday, on a big board, and the café answers every move. Then the blitz. A declared exception to the motion rules, on these terms:
- **Files:** `sections/Chess.tsx` (the heading, the still board, the four payoffs, the blitz), `styles/sections/chess.css`, and `sections/chess/*`: `views.tsx` (the piece sprite, the board, the transcript, the button: no hooks, drawn by the server and the game alike), `model.ts` (the whole puzzle as a pure reducer, tested in `model.test.ts`), `PuzzleIsland`, `Game` and `ChessMotion`.
- **Rules:** `src/lib/chess.ts` has no imports and only erasable TypeScript, so the pipeline imports it with Node's type stripping. Keep it that way. Its move generator is proven by perft (`src/lib/chess.test.ts`).
- **Puzzles:** `src/content/puzzles.ts` is generated by `npm run assets:puzzles` from the Lichess puzzle database (CC0): mates in two from real games, White to move, rating 1000 to 1400, at most 18 pieces. The script and `src/lib/puzzle.test.ts` check every one with the page's solver (one key only, no mate in one, a mate after every defence that isn't an underpromotion). The week turns over at Monday 00:00, Moscow time, counted from `FIRST_MONDAY`; the list goes round. A rerun reshuffles the calendar; saves are keyed by week and id, so nothing breaks.
- **The board:** layers from the bottom: the checker, the marks (milk tints), the coordinates inside the edge squares, the pieces (flat silhouettes: milk with ink seams, ink with milk outlines), the café's arrow, and on top a transparent `role="grid"` of buttons with one tab stop (arrows, Home, End, Enter or Space, Escape). Click, then click; a mouse or pen can drag; touch never drags, so the page keeps scrolling. Pieces move on CSS `translate`, a drag on `transform`.
- **Colour:** the café's latest move is a tomato arc, the section's one accent; it goes when the puzzle is solved and the grade takes over.
- **Voice:** the transcript is problem-column notation in Russian letters («1. Лd1? Сb7!», «1. Лb3+! Крa6», «2. Лa4#»), and the café's lines are only «Мата нет.», «Пат.» and «Мат.». After the mate it asks «Как дела?» and the grade lands as that answer's slip: no slips «10 из 10», one slip or the hint «Всё шик», more «Нормально», the solution shown «Так себе». Under it, that answer's reply (the section's one italic moment) and its dish's price, as in the hero.
- **Motion:** pieces slide; a wrong move is answered after 420ms and taken back 1.1s later (a press takes it back at once); Black's last move slides in once as the board comes into view (not after a capture, not in reduced motion, not when the board is already on screen); the grade lands (the hero's landing, as CSS keyframes on a wrapper, never on the slip's `rotate`); the heading and the blitz rise quietly.
- **Loading:** `PuzzleIsland` shows the server's still board of the build's week until the game (`Game`, the rules, the puzzles) arrives with `import()` near the section. The game never hydrates: it reads the date and the saved solve in `boot()`, never during render. The island's imports of it stay type-only.
- **Without JS:** the build's week on a still board (`role="img"`, the pieces in words), «1. ?» and the whole blitz; the button is hidden.
- **Storage:** `localStorage["kd-puzzle"] = {"v":1,"week":n,"id":"…","grade":"…","line":[key, reply, mate]}`, replayed and checked on read, kept until Monday; «Решить заново» clears it.

## Обратная связь: the note (`/feedback`)
The guest's turn in the conversation, set as the hero is: the café's pad on the left, the café itself on the right. A declared exception to the motion rules, on these terms:
- **Files:** `src/app/feedback/page.tsx` (the heading), `src/app/feedback/actions.ts` (the server action), `src/components/feedback/*`: `NoteDesk` (the pad and the café's side, the one island), `NoteRows` (the note's printed rows, as a form or as the torn-off sheet), `Loop` (a pen loop), `flight.ts` (the tear), `Asked` and `AskedMotion` («Уже спрашивали.»). The note's rules are `src/lib/feedback.ts`, its pen `src/lib/loop.ts` (both tested in `src/lib/feedback.test.ts`); its words are `src/content/feedback.ts` and `t.feedback`. Styles: `src/styles/sections/feedback.css`.
- **The pad:** the notice's paper, a box-shadow (never a filter: it would redraw on every keystroke), the plain wordmark on the letterhead asking «Как дела?», the notice's perforation, and two more sheets' edges under the top one. The sheet is a printed form: a label (`.label`) over each row, the tones in the display face, the cafés' streets, the words on ruled lines in their own pitch, a signature and a contact on lines to write on.
- **One scale:** the pad and the landed note are each a size container and everything on the sheet is sized in its `cqi` (`--px`, `--fs-tone`, `--fs-text`, `--fs-lab`), so the note on the café's side is the sheet, only smaller. The pad keeps floors for phones (16px text never zooms iOS); the note drops them from 1024. Layout choices that depend on width (the tones on one line, the signature beside the contact) are forced alike from 1024, so the two are always set the same way.
- **Pen loops:** choosing a tone or a café draws a loop round it in ink (`penLoop`, seeded per option, drawn in with `stroke-dashoffset`; CSS only, so it works before hydration and without JS). No other circling, no red loops. A second click rubs it out: both are optional.
- **The café's side:** sticky beside the pad from 1024. Whom the note goes to (the circled café's street and `CafeStatus`, or «Все кофейни · город»), that café's photo (all nine stacked, CSS crossfades them; the city's flagship when none is circled), then the café's voice (`.reply`, the section's one italic moment: the tone's line while the guest writes, the reply once the note has gone) and «Если срочно» with the café's number. All the lines are stacked in one cell, so nothing jumps.
- **Colour:** the letterhead's tomato clover, and tomato ink only for what to put right. Focus rings as everywhere.
- **Motion** (full motion only), beyond the rules above: the loops draw in; the café's line changes as the hero's answer does; sending tears the sheet off at the perforation: a copy of the landed note, laid out at the sheet's own width, comes away from the stub right corner first, is carried over in an arc with a little turn in the air and scaled down (never up: text scaled up goes soft), and lands as the hero's tab does (26px up, 5% larger, `kd.settle`). It follows the note's live position, so the page can scroll under it (phones, where the café's side sits under the pad). The pad gives as the sheet comes away; the stub (`tornSheet(id).stub`, the other half of the note's rip) stays under the perforation; the café's reply rises once the note lands. Reduced motion: the note is simply there.
- **After sending:** a fresh sheet waits on the pad. Phones fold it under the stub with «Написать ещё» so the note and the reply come up under the pad; on a desktop the guest can write again at once, and the café's side lets the old note go at the first edit.
- **The form:** a server action through `useActionState`. With JS the page checks the note (`checkNote`), then sends it itself in a transition, so React never resets the form (a refused note keeps its circles). Without JS it posts as it stands and the page comes back with the note and the reply, or the note as it was with what to put right; the city switch hides and both cities' cafés show under their names. The action checks every note again, drops a filled honeypot, and hands the note on (`deliver`: a plain-text letter to the operator's mailbox at a mail provider in Russia, from `FEEDBACK_SMTP_URL`, `FEEDBACK_MAIL_TO` and optionally `FEEDBACK_MAIL_FROM`; without them a development build logs it and a production build says it could not send). The letter carries the consent record: the note's number, the Moscow time and the consent's edition.
- **Consent and contact** (see «Персональные данные» below): nothing is sent without the guest's own tick in the consent box (`consent`, checked on the page, by the browser without JS, and in the action). The box is never ticked for the guest; the consent's text and the policy are linked beside it, in a new tab so the note is kept. The contact is a phone or an email only: a Telegram name is recognised and turned down with a reason, because an answer through a foreign messenger would carry the guest's data abroad. The landed note prints its number in its corner, for a request about the data.
- **Focus:** a refused note focuses its first field to put right; a sent one focuses the café's reply (`tabIndex={-1}`), so a screen reader hears it while the paper is in the air.

## Персональные данные: the policy and the consent (`/privacy`, `/privacy/consent`)
The documents 152-ФЗ asks of a site that collects personal data: the policy (art. 18.1 part 2), published on every page through the footer and beside the form, and the consent, set out separately from anything else (art. 9 part 1, since 01.09.2025) and given by its own tick on the note.
- **Files:** the text is data in `src/content/privacy.ts` (`policy`, `consent`, `consentIntro`, `brief`, `deviceStorage`, `operator`, `EDITION`), set by `src/components/privacy/LegalDoc.tsx` (with the contents' `TocSpy`) on `src/app/privacy/page.tsx` and `src/app/privacy/consent/page.tsx`. Styles: `src/styles/sections/privacy.css`. Tests: `src/lib/privacy.test.ts`.
- **They tell the truth about the code.** Change what the site stores, sends or collects, and change the documents in the same commit: the tests fail when a browser storage key is added or dropped without the table, when a cookie, analytics or an iframe appears, when geolocation is asked for elsewhere, and when the form's action sends a note abroad.
- **The operator's details** are `{tokens}` in the text (`{name}`, `{inn}`, `{email}`…), filled from `operator`. Until one is known it is drawn as a blank on a form: a line with what goes on it printed under it, heard as «ИНН: будет указано». The footer shows the operator's details (149-ФЗ art. 10 part 2) once `operator.name` is set.
- **One edition:** `EDITION` and `EDITION_LABEL` date both documents, and the note's letter quotes it as the consent's version. A change to the text is a new edition.
- **Set as a document:** the heading and lead, the contents on cols 1–3 (sticky; `TocSpy` marks the clause being read with `aria-current`), the clauses on cols 5–11 at most 62ch, paragraph numbers («3.2.») hanging in the margin from 1024. The policy opens with «Коротко», six plain lines on a torn note of the notice's paper with pen ticks. Legal text binds a reference to its number («статьи 18.1») and a number to what it counts («24 часов»). No motion. It prints clean: the chrome, the contents and the grain drop out.

## Accessibility
- One `h1` (the hero), an `h2` per section via `DialogueHeading`, real lists and landmarks. Alt text comes from `photos.ts`.
- Visible focus is global: a 2px `--focus` ring with a 3px offset. Never remove it; inside anything that clips, or on the screen edge, use a negative `outline-offset`.
- Touch targets are at least 44px. Decorative SVG is `aria-hidden`.
- External links get `target="_blank" rel="noreferrer"` and the sr-only «(откроется в новой вкладке)».
- The page works without JS: every block renders on the server, the city prices fall back to Нальчик, the hero's checked radio picks the panel, and the reviews' pile shows its first note (its arrows wait for JS).
- One exception: without JS the runner can't start, so its block is hidden (`html:not(.js)`), like the header's «Разделы».
